Verb Deep-Dive: wollen (to want)

Desire and intention — plus a distinct pattern for wanting someone ELSE to do something, and a nostalgic "used to want to."
🎬 "Wollen" — Street Interview Sentences ↗
wollen is a modal verb — same mechanics as können and müssen. Attach it to a second verb pushed to the end of the sentence: Ich will schwimmen gehen. But wollen has an extra trick the other modals don't need as often: wanting someone else to do something uses a completely different structure — covered below, since it's one of the more common mistakes English speakers make with this verb.
Basic skeleton: [subject] + [wollen, conjugated] ... rest of sentence ... [main verb, infinitive]
① want to (do something yourself) ② want that... (someone else) ③ Willst du...? (invitations) ④ wollte mal (used to want to)
