Verb Deep-Dive: sollen (should / to be supposed to)

Instructions passed on from someone else, softer advice, helpless questions, and one very common colloquial idiom.
🎬 "Sollen" — 5 Key Meanings Explained ↗
sollen is a modal verb — same mechanics as können, müssen, and wollen. The core difference from müssen: müssen is an obligation you feel yourself; sollen is an instruction or expectation that came from someone else — a boss, a rule, a recommendation, a third party.
Skeleton: [subject] + [sollen, conjugated] ... rest of sentence ... [main verb, infinitive] — e.g. Du sollst pünktlich sein.
① instruction (do this) ② recommendation (softer) ③ was soll ich...? (helpless) ④ sollte (advice / unfulfilled wish) ⑤ was soll das? (colloquial)
