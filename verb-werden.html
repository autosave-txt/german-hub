Verb Deep-Dive: werden (to become)

The third essential verb — and the busiest one. Four completely different jobs, one verb.
🎬 Source video: "Werden" — Every Form Explained ↗
werden means "to become" on its own — but its real power is as a helper verb. It builds the future tense (with sein/haben), the entire passive voice (with every other verb), a way to express a guess or assumption, and — in its würde form — the everyday conditional ("would ___"). Four jobs, one irregular verb. This page covers each separately so it doesn't blur together.
