A1 Speaking Test Guide

Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 — everything you need for the speaking exam in one place.

1. Official Exam Structure

Group exam · max 4 candidates · 2 examiners · ≈ 15 minutes · no preparation time.

Part 1 — Sich vorstellen (≈ 1 min per person)

You introduce yourself from a keyword card (Name, Alter, Land, Wohnort, Sprachen, Beruf, Hobby). Then the examiner asks you to spell something and say a number.

Part 2 — Fragen stellen und beantworten

You get topic cards. You ask simple questions and answer the other candidates’ questions.

Part 3 — Bitten formulieren

You make simple polite requests to the other people using prompt cards.

Key point: Part 1 is a short monologue from the card. Follow-ups are only spelling and numbers.

2. Official Examiner Instructions (Part 1)

The oral examination is a group exam with a maximum of four candidates. It consists of three parts. The examination is conducted by two examiners. The moderator makes all the announcements and asks follow-up questions if necessary when an utterance is unclear or incomplete. The assessor evaluates the candidates’ performance and aligns the assessment with the moderator at the end.

Exam objective: Give important personal information in the simplest form.

Exam format: A sheet with keywords is visible to everyone.

Announcement: “We would like to get to know each other. Tell us: Who are you? Here are a few words to help you. First I will introduce myself.”

Candidates introduce themselves one after another. At the end the examiner asks each person to spell something and give a number.

4. Part 1 — How to Answer the Card

Ideal length: 30–45 seconds. One piece of information per sentence. No stories.

  • Do not talk longer than 45–50 seconds.
  • Do not explain your name or tell life stories.
  • Do not use complicated sentences.

7. Part 2 — Questions & Answers (with English)

You receive a topic card. Ask one question and answer one question. Keep every sentence short.

Essen und Trinken (Food and Drink)

Questions
Was essen Sie gerne? → What do you like to eat?
Was trinken Sie gerne? → What do you like to drink?
Essen Sie Fleisch? → Do you eat meat?
Was essen Sie zum Frühstück? → What do you eat for breakfast?

Answers
Ich esse gerne Pizza / Brot / Obst / Reis. → I like to eat pizza / bread / fruit / rice.
Ich trinke gerne Wasser / Tee / Kaffee / Saft. → I like to drink water / tea / coffee / juice.
Nein, ich esse kein Fleisch. → No, I don’t eat meat.
Ja, ich esse gerne Fleisch. → Yes, I like to eat meat.
Zum Frühstück esse ich Brot und Käse. → For breakfast I eat bread and cheese.

Freizeit / Hobbys (Free time / Hobbies)

Questions
Was machen Sie in Ihrer Freizeit? → What do you do in your free time?
Haben Sie Hobbys? → Do you have hobbies?
Spielen Sie Sport? → Do you play sports?

Answers
In meiner Freizeit spiele ich Fußball / lese ich / höre ich Musik. → In my free time I play football / I read / I listen to music.
Ja, mein Hobby ist Musik / Sport / Lesen. → Yes, my hobby is music / sport / reading.
Ja, ich spiele gerne Fußball. → Yes, I like to play football.
Nein, ich spiele keinen Sport. → No, I don’t play any sport.

Wochenende (Weekend)

Questions
Was machen Sie am Wochenende? → What do you do at the weekend?
Treffen Sie Freunde am Wochenende? → Do you meet friends at the weekend?

Answers
Am Wochenende treffe ich Freunde / bleibe ich zu Hause / gehe ich spazieren. → At the weekend I meet friends / I stay at home / I go for a walk.
Ja, ich treffe oft Freunde. → Yes, I often meet friends.
Nein, ich bleibe meistens zu Hause. → No, I usually stay at home.

Einkaufen (Shopping)

Questions
Wo kaufen Sie ein? → Where do you shop?
Was kaufen Sie gerne? → What do you like to buy?

Answers
Ich kaufe im Supermarkt ein. → I shop at the supermarket.
Ich kaufe online. → I shop online.
Ich kaufe gerne Obst und Brot. → I like to buy fruit and bread.

11. 10-Day Drill Plan

Day 1–2: Memorise your personal Part 1 introduction (7 sentences). Say it 20 times out loud each day.
Day 3: Alphabet with pronunciation + numbers 0–100. Spell your full name and a phone number 15 times.
Day 4: Record full Part 1 + spelling + number. Listen and fix every hesitation.
Day 5: Learn all Part 2 question/answer blocks (Essen, Freizeit, Wochenende, Einkaufen).
Day 6: Learn all Part 3 requests and responses.
Day 7: Full simulation of all three parts using the scripts. Record it.
Day 8: Watch the official sample video, then immediately repeat the same parts yourself.
Day 9: Another full recorded simulation. Compare Day 7 vs Day 9.
Day 10: Final run under exam conditions (no notes, timer, speak clearly).

Success criterion: You can produce your Part 1 introduction, spell your name, give a number, ask and answer one simple question, and make one polite request without notes and without long pauses.

All language stays inside A1 range and is based on the official Goethe Modellsatz, Übungssätze and the official sample video. Work the drill plan. This page contains every phrase you need for the speaking exam.
