Today’s destination

By the end of Day 1, you will be able to…

Present tense is the active target. Past and future forms are introduced early for recognition and pattern-building, not full memorization.

  • Describe your morning in roughly 10–15 connected German sentences with this page as light support.
  • Sequence actions naturally with morgens, zuerst, dann, danach, später and zum Schluss.
  • Keep V2 word order when a time word comes first: Morgens mache ich Kaffee.
  • Use common morning verbs in the present and recognize their useful person forms: ich, du, er/sie/es, wir, ihr, sie/Sie.
  • Recognize separable/reflexive chunks such as aufstehen, sich anziehen, sich abtrocknen and sich die Zähne putzen.
  • Choose the right everyday verb: aufwachen / aufstehen, machen / trinken / haben, gehen / fahren.
  • Talk about real variation: early/late, with or without breakfast, bus/on foot, rushed/slow, weekday/weekend.
  • Recognize time shifts in familiar routine sentences: present, spoken past (Perfekt) and future meaning.

Patterns you should notice

Day 1 grammar map

Enough explanation to make the input intelligible. We are not trying to finish German grammar today.

1 · Basic statement

SUBJECT + VERB + REST
Ich + trinke + Kaffee.

The conjugated verb is in position 2.

2 · Time first → “Yoda” V2

TIME + VERB + SUBJECT + REST
Morgens + trinke + ich + Kaffee.

Putting Morgens, Heute, Danach, etc. first does not move the verb away from position 2. The subject moves behind it.

3 · Separable verbs

Ich stehe um sieben Uhr AUF.
Ich ziehe mich AN.

In a normal main clause, the prefix separates and goes to the end. Learn the full verb too: aufstehen, anziehen.

4 · nicht vs. kein

Ich frühstücke NICHT.
Ich trinke KEINEN Kaffee.

nicht negates the action/statement. Forms of kein negate a noun. You will systematize the endings later.

5 · Destination chunks

in die Küche · ins Badezimmer · zur Arbeit

These article changes come from case. For Day 1, learn these highly useful combinations as chunks instead of stopping to derive every ending.

6 · Body-care chunks

Ich putze mir die Zähne.
Ich wasche mir das Gesicht.

The literal grammar is different from English. Do not force an English word-for-word pattern onto it. Learn the whole expression first; the dative explanation can come later.

7 · German nouns

der Kaffee · die Küche · das Handy

Learn the article with the noun. The page's tap card will show it whenever that noun is in the lexicon.

8 · Capitalization

Kaffee · Küche · Arbeit · Morgen

German nouns are capitalized. morgen = tomorrow; der Morgen = the morning; morgens = in the morning.

Early tense exposure

The same morning across time

Do not memorize every past form today. The goal is to see that the same familiar action can be moved through time without becoming a completely new sentence.

Active todayPräsens: produce present-tense routine sentences confidently.
Recognize todayPerfekt: the normal everyday spoken past for many actions.
Notice todayFuture meaning: German often uses present tense + a future time word; werden + infinitive also exists.
Important: Morgen gehe ich zur Arbeit. already means “Tomorrow I’ll go to work.” German does not require an explicit future tense every time English uses “will.”

End-of-day target

What “done” looks like

By the end of Day 1, a good result is not “I memorized this whole page.” A good result is:

  • You can read the baseline routine with only occasional taps.
  • You recognize morgens, zuerst, dann, danach automatically.
  • You understand why Morgens mache ich... has the verb before ich.
  • You can distinguish aufwachen / aufstehen, machen / trinken / haben, and gehen / fahren.
  • You can say or write roughly 10–15 sentences about your own morning using the material above, with the page still available as support.
  • You recognize familiar routine sentences in Perfekt and understand that future meaning often uses present tense + a future time word.
