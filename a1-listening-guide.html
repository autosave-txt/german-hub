A1 Listening Test Guide

Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 — Hören. Everything you need to score high on the listening part.

1. Official Structure

Time: ≈ 20 minutes · Max points: 15 · Three parts.

Teil 1 — Short dialogues (6 questions)

You hear 6 short everyday conversations (shop, street, work, home, etc.). For each dialogue there is one multiple-choice question (a / b / c). Each text is played twice.

Teil 2 — Announcements (4 questions)

You hear 4 short public announcements (train station, airport, school, etc.). Each has one Richtig / Falsch statement. These are usually played only once.

Teil 3 — Messages / short texts (5 questions)

You hear 5 short messages or announcements (phone message, radio, information desk). Multiple choice a / b / c. Each text is played twice.

2. How the Exam Works

  • You first read the questions (you have a short time before the audio starts).
  • Then the recording plays.
  • You mark your answers while listening or right after.
  • At the end you transfer answers to the answer sheet (a few minutes are given).

Most important skill: read the question carefully BEFORE the audio starts so you know exactly what to listen for.

3. What You Need to Catch

A1 listening almost always asks for very concrete information:

  • Times and days (Wann? Um wie viel Uhr?)
  • Places (Wo? Wohin?)
  • Prices and numbers
  • Who does what
  • What someone should do (especially with “sollen”)
  • Simple reasons (Warum?)

4. Example Audio Tasks (with transcripts + English)

Teil 1 Example — Short dialogue

Question: Wo ist der Bahnhof?
a) links   b) rechts   c) geradeaus

Audio (played twice):
A: Entschuldigung, wo ist der Bahnhof?
B: Der Bahnhof? Gehen Sie hier geradeaus, dann links.

A: Excuse me, where is the station?
B: The station? Go straight ahead here, then left.

Answer: a) links (the final direction given is left)

Teil 2 Example — Announcement (Richtig/Falsch)

Statement: Die Fahrgäste sollen im Zug bleiben. → Richtig / Falsch?

Audio (often once only):
“Achtung, Achtung. Der Zug nach München hat 10 Minuten Verspätung. Bitte bleiben Sie im Zug.”

“Attention. The train to Munich is delayed by 10 minutes. Please stay on the train.”

Answer: Richtig

Teil 3 Example — Phone message

Question: Wann soll Frau Krause anrufen?
a) am Vormittag   b) zwischen 13 und 18 Uhr   c) nach 18 Uhr

Audio (played twice):
“Guten Tag, hier ist das Büro von Herrn Schneider. Bitte rufen Sie zwischen 13 und 18 Uhr zurück. Danke.”

“Good day, this is Mr Schneider’s office. Please call back between 1 pm and 6 pm. Thank you.”

Answer: b) zwischen 13 und 18 Uhr

6. Strategy for Each Part

Teil 1 (dialogues – played twice)

Read the question and the three options before the first listening. On the first play, try to catch the answer. On the second play, confirm it. If you still don’t know, eliminate the options that are clearly wrong.

Teil 2 (announcements – often once only)

These are short and fast. Read the Richtig/Falsch statement carefully. Focus on the key word (time, place, “sollen”, “nicht”). One clear piece of information decides the answer.

Teil 3 (messages – played twice)

Same approach as Teil 1. The language is usually a bit more formal (phone messages, official announcements). Listen for the exact detail the question asks for.

7. Numbers, Times and Days — Must Be Automatic

You will hear numbers and times constantly. You must recognise them instantly:

0–20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100
halb (half past) · Viertel nach / Viertel vor
heute · morgen · übermorgen · gestern
Montag … Sonntag

If numbers and times are still slow for you, practise them every day for 5–10 minutes until they are automatic.

8. Practice Plan

Day 1–2: Master numbers 0–100 and telling the time (including halb / Viertel).
Day 3–4: Practise Teil 1 style dialogues. Always read the question first, then listen twice.
Day 5–6: Practise Teil 2 announcements (Richtig/Falsch). Focus on “sollen” and key details.
Day 7–8: Practise Teil 3 messages. Use official audio when possible.
Day 9–10: Full 20-minute listening practice under exam conditions with official Modellsatz / Übungssatz audio.
