A1 Writing Test Guide

Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 — Schreiben. Everything you need to score high on the writing part.

1. Official Structure

Time: ≈ 20 minutes · Max points: 15 · Two tasks only.

Teil 1 — Formular ausfüllen (5 points)

You read a short situation (4–6 sentences). Then you fill in 5 missing pieces of information on a form (name, number of people, payment method, place, date, etc.). Each correct field = 1 point.

Teil 2 — Kurze Mitteilung (10 points)

You write a short message / email / note of about 30 words. You must cover exactly three given points. You also need a greeting and a closing. Scoring: up to 3 points per content point + 1 point for proper greeting/closing.

This is the most predictable part of the whole A1 exam. The language stays extremely simple.

2. Teil 1 — How to Fill the Form

Read the short text carefully. All the answers are hidden inside it. Transfer the information exactly into the form fields.

Typical form fields you will see:

  • Vorname / Nachname (first name / last name)
  • Anzahl der Personen / Davon Kinder (number of people / of which children)
  • PLZ / Ort (postcode / town)
  • Datum / Tag (date / day)
  • Zahlungsweise: Bar / Kreditkarte / Überweisung (payment: cash / credit card / bank transfer)
  • Telefonnummer / E-Mail
  • Straße + Hausnummer

Key rule: Write only what is asked. Spelling must be correct. Dates are written day.month.year (e.g. 15.03.1990).

Concrete example

Situation text:
Ihre Freundin Eva Müller macht mit ihrem Mann und ihren zwei Söhnen (8 und 11 Jahre) Urlaub in Seeheim.
Sie bucht für Sonntag eine Busfahrt. Eva hat keine Kreditkarte.

Your friend Eva Müller is on holiday in Seeheim with her husband and their two sons (8 and 11 years old). She books a bus trip for Sunday. Eva does not have a credit card.
Form fields → correct answers:
Anzahl der Personen: 4 (Eva + husband + 2 sons)
Davon Kinder: 2
Ort: Seeheim
Tag: Sonntag
Zahlungsweise: Bar (no credit card → cash)

3. Teil 2 — How to Write the Short Message

Always use this fixed structure:

1. Greeting
2. One sentence that answers point 1
3. One sentence that answers point 2
4. One sentence that answers point 3
5. Closing + your name

Target length: 25–40 words. More words = more chances to make mistakes. Cover every point clearly.

Greetings (Anrede)

Informal (friend / classmate): Hallo Anna, / Liebe Anna,
Formal (office / information / teacher): Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren, / Sehr geehrte Frau Müller, / Sehr geehrter Herr Schmidt,

Closings (Gruß)

Informal: Viele Grüße / Liebe Grüße + your first name
Formal: Mit freundlichen Grüßen + full name

4. Model Answers (Learn These Patterns)

Model A — Formal (Tourist Information)

Task: You visit Dresden in August. Ask for culture programme + hotel addresses.

Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren,

ich besuche Dresden im August.
Bitte schicken Sie mir Informationen über Museen und Theater.
Können Sie mir auch Hoteladressen schicken?

Mit freundlichen Grüßen
John Smith

≈ 32 words. All three points covered. Greeting + closing present.

Model B — Informal (to a friend)

Task: You are sick and cannot come to the course. Say why + when you return.

Hallo Maria,

ich bin krank und kann heute nicht zum Kurs kommen.
Ich habe Fieber.
Ich komme am Montag wieder.

Viele Grüße
John

Short, clear, all points answered.

Model C — Invitation

Task: Invite a friend to your birthday. Give day, time and place.

Liebe Anna,

ich habe am Samstag Geburtstag.
Die Party ist um 18 Uhr bei mir zu Hause.
Kannst du bitte kommen?

Liebe Grüße
John

6. Scoring — How to Get Full Marks

Teil 1 (5 points): Each correct field = 1 point. Spelling counts.

Teil 2 (10 points):

  • 3 content points × up to 3 points each = 9 points
  • Greeting + closing correctly present = 1 point

If a point is missing or completely unclear → 0 for that point. Simple correct sentences beat complicated wrong ones every time.

7. Practice Plan

Day 1–2: Memorise the formal and informal greeting + closing pairs.
Day 3–4: Write the three model messages above from memory. Check every point is covered.
Day 5–6: Practise form-filling: take any short German paragraph and extract 5 pieces of information into a blank form.
Day 7–8: Write 5 new short messages on common topics (illness, invitation, asking for information, cancelling, thanking).
Day 9–10: Timed practice: 20 minutes for both tasks. No notes.
