Verb Deep-Dive: müssen (must / to have to)

Obligation, necessity, and — in Konjunktiv II — a softer "really should."
🎬 "Müssen" — Every Tense Explained ↗  ·  "Müssen" — Street Interview Sentences ↗
müssen is a modal verb — same mechanics as können. It attaches a meaning (here: necessity or obligation) to a second verb, which gets pushed to the end of the sentence in its plain infinitive form.
The skeleton: [subject] + [müssen, conjugated] ... rest of sentence ... [main verb, infinitive] — e.g. Ich muss jetzt gehen. Just like können, müssen can stand alone with no second verb when the action is obvious: Ich muss gar nichts. ("I don't have to do anything at all.")
