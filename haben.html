Verb Deep-Dive: haben (to have)

Every tense, plus its main job as the default helper verb for the Perfekt tense.
🎬 Video 1 ↗  ·  Video 2 ↗
haben is sein's constant companion — together they're the two verbs every other verb in the Perfekt (past) tense leans on. Where sein only handles a small group of movement/change-of-state verbs, haben handles almost everything else. It's irregular but more forgiving than sein — several forms (hat, habt) are close to the infinitive.
