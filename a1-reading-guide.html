A1 Reading Test Guide

Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1 — Lesen. Everything you need to score high on the reading part.

1. Official Structure

Time: 25 minutes · Max points: 15 · Three parts.

Teil 1 — Short texts (emails / notes) → Richtig / Falsch

You read two short texts (usually emails, SMS, or notes from friends/colleagues). There are 5 statements in total. For each statement you decide Richtig (true) or Falsch (false). The information is always in the text — do not use outside knowledge.

Teil 2 — Matching situations to notices

You get 5 situations (“You want to…”) and several short notices / advertisements / websites. For each situation you choose which notice fits (usually a or b). This tests whether you can find the right information quickly.

Teil 3 — Signs and short notices → Richtig / Falsch

You see short everyday signs, notices, or information boards (at a school, hotel, station, restaurant, etc.). 5 statements. Mark Richtig or Falsch.

2. How to Approach Every Part

Golden rule: Read the question / statement first, then look for the exact information in the text. Do not try to understand every single word.

  • Underline key words in the question (time, place, number, name, “not”, “only”, etc.).
  • Scan the text for those same words or clear synonyms.
  • If the statement says something the text does not say → Falsch.
  • If you are unsure, still choose an answer — there is no penalty for wrong answers.

3. Typical Text Types You Will See

  • Short emails and SMS between friends or colleagues
  • Invitations and cancellations
  • Hotel / course / event notices
  • Opening times, prices, rules on signs
  • Small advertisements (flat to rent, language course, etc.)
  • Information boards at stations, museums, restaurants

5. Strategy for Each Part

Teil 1 (emails / notes)

Read the two texts once quickly. Then read statement 1, find the answer, mark it, move to statement 2. Do not re-read the whole text for every question.

Teil 2 (matching)

Read the situation first (“Sie möchten…”) and underline what you need. Then look only at the notices for that exact information. One notice can be the answer for only one situation.

Teil 3 (signs)

These are very short. Look for numbers, times, “nicht”, “nur”, “ab”, “bis”. Most mistakes happen when people miss a small negative word.

6. Common Traps

  • The text says “Montag bis Freitag” but the statement says “am Samstag” → Falsch
  • The text gives a price for adults, the statement talks about children → check carefully
  • A notice says “Eintritt frei für Studenten” — the statement says “Eintritt ist immer frei” → Falsch
  • Times: “ab 18 Uhr” means from 18:00 onwards, not until 18:00

7. Practice Plan

Day 1–2: Learn the high-frequency vocabulary list above until you recognise every word instantly.
Day 3–4: Do Teil 1 practice (short emails). Focus only on finding the information, not translating everything.
Day 5–6: Practise Teil 2 matching tasks. Time yourself.
Day 7–8: Practise Teil 3 signs and notices. Pay special attention to “nicht / nur / ab / bis”.
Day 9–10: Full 25-minute reading practice under exam conditions using official Übungssätze.
