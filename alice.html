Alice im Wunderland — Ganz Einfach

A very simplified original German retelling — 10 short scenes. Tap or hover any word for its meaning.
This is not a translation of Lewis Carroll's book — it's a short, original retelling in extremely simple German, written to be readable at a very early stage while still following the real story beats (rabbit hole, shrink/grow, tea party, the Queen, waking up). The book is public domain, so borrowing the story shape is fine — the sentences themselves are freshly written, deliberately kept as short and repetitive as possible.
