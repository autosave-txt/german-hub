Verb Deep-Dive: können (can / to be able to)

Ability, possibility, and — very often — the polite way to ask someone for something.
🎬 "Können" — Every Tense Explained ↗  ·  "Können" — 40 Street Interview Sentences ↗
können is a modal verb — it doesn't act alone. Instead of describing an action itself, it attaches a meaning (ability, possibility, permission) to a second verb, which gets pushed to the very end of the sentence in its plain infinitive form.
The skeleton: [subject] + [können, conjugated] ... rest of sentence ... [main verb, infinitive] — e.g. Ich kann gut Deutsch sprechen. können can also stand completely alone with no second verb at all, when the action is obvious from context: Ich kann das. ("I can [do] that.")
