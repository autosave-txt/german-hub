Verb Deep-Dive: dürfen (may / to be allowed to)

Permission, prohibition, elegant polite questions, gratitude for an opportunity, and polite guesses.
🎬 "Dürfen" — 5 Meanings Explained ↗  ·  "Dürfen" — Street Interview Sentences ↗
dürfen completes the modal set alongside können, müssen, wollen, and sollen. Its core meaning is permission — but German stretches it into a few genuinely different directions, from polite questions to expressing gratitude for an opportunity.
Skeleton: [subject] + [dürfen, conjugated] ... rest of sentence ... [main verb, infinitive] — e.g. Ich darf heute später kommen.
① permission (may / allowed to) ② nicht dürfen (forbidden) ③ Darf ich...? (elegant question) ④ ich durfte (had the honor/pleasure) ⑤ dürfte (polite guess)
