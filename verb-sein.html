Verb Deep-Dive: sein (to be)

Every tense, plus the two jobs "sein" does in German — main verb and helper verb.
🎬 Source video: "Sein" — Every Tense Explained ↗
sein is the single most important verb in German — it's the most common verb overall, and unlike almost every other verb it's irregular in every tense, so there's no shortcut: it just has to be memorized. The payoff is high, though — once you know all of these forms cold, you've covered a huge share of everyday sentences.
